Terms & Privacy Policy

1. Account Registration & Security

Comfort Factory maintains high cryptographic standards for customer authentication. Passwords are salted and hashed using PBKDF2/SHA-512 with 100,000 iterations and never stored in plain text.

2. Indian Data Protection Compliance

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, your registered mobile number, email, and delivery addresses are exclusively utilized for order fulfillment, dispatch logistics, and invoice generation.

3. Customer Rights (Portability & Erasure)

You may download a complete copy of your customer records or request permanent deletion of your account at any time through your Customer Account Dashboard.

4. Delivery & Order Processing

Orders are dispatched with verified logistical partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC) with tracking updates provided via your registered contact details.
